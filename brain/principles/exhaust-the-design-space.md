# Exhaust the Design Space

For consequential decisions without a clear precedent, compare multiple concrete alternatives before committing to one.

**Why:** The first coherent design often reflects the order ideas arrived rather than the best tradeoff. Cheap competing sketches expose assumptions and make the decision falsifiable.

**Pattern:**
- Name the decision and evaluation criteria before exploring.
- Produce two or three materially different candidates.
- Compare them on the real surface with the same constraints.
- Choose one, record why, and discard the others.

**Guardrail:** Do not fan out routine or reversible choices. Exploration earns its cost only when alternatives could change user experience, architecture, or operational risk.

**Test:** Can you explain what evidence would make a rejected candidate win? If not, the comparison was decorative.
